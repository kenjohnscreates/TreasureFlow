What site builders dislike about LLM UI

  • Homogeneity, not broken CSS. Models complete blue-500 and Inter. Sailop’s 2026 scan of ~1,000 AI sites: generic scores rose over six months, worst in color and type.
  • The starter pack: purple/indigo gradients, Inter/Geist, centered hero, three identical rounded cards, glass/glow, gradient text on metrics, transition-all duration-300, nested cards, thick colored side borders.
  • 2026 “tasteful” reset is also a tell: cream ground, sage accent, Instrument Serif / Fraunces. Swapping purple for Anthropic house style is not a direction.
  • Layout by template (nav, hero, features, testimonials, CTA) instead of by job. Fake metrics and testimonials. Weak hierarchy. No real conversion mechanics.
  • Cardocalypse, giant Lucide icons, Japanese-style redundant UX writing, accessibility misses (contrast, focus).

Sources: Sailop 7 dimensions, 2026 scan, Impeccable slop, Unslop UI, Paul Bakaus slop tells.

What we borrowed from live products

  • Mercury. Total cash first, accounts under it, settings that read as software not a bank. Light canvas. Color only for state.
  • Morpho app. Gray ground, white rows, rate on the right, filters as text not icon candy. Calm DeFi, not neon.
  • Aave app. Dark chrome, dense numerals, a sheet of positions. We kept density, dropped the violet CTA.
  • Wise / Stripe (from design writeups). Show the number a competitor would hide. Fees, caps, and rejection reasons before commit. Tables over sparkline decoration.

PRD on every mock

  • 0:40 Manual sweep. addLiquidity hash or “below buffer, no sweep”.
  • 1:40 “send 2,400 USDC to 0x...”. Shortfall, removeLiquidity, transfer.
  • 2:40 “send 50,000”. Policy reject. Nothing moved.
  • 3:20 Three Flash cbBTC limits at 2 / 4 / 6 percent below spot. No performance claim.
  • 4:10 Stretch narrated. US machine. Bankr custody split labeled.
  • Copy: trailing fee yield only. Daily cap called out as orchestrator. No em dashes.
